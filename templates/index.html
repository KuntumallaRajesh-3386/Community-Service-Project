Creating awareness for a healthier society by educating people about food safety.

About The Issue

Cooking oil adulteration is the intentional mixing of pure edible oils with cheaper, low-quality, or harmful substances to increase profits. These substances may include mineral oils, reused cooking oils, artificial colours, or low-grade vegetable oils. Such adulteration reduces the nutritional value, quality, and safety of edible oils, making them unsafe for regular consumption.
